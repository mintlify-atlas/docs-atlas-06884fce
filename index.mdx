SlimBrave Neo uses Chromium's built-in enterprise policy system to debloat and harden Brave Browser without browser extensions, binary modifications, or external dependencies. Pick a preset or hand-tune 79 individual policies across 7 categories — then apply in one click or one command.

## What SlimBrave Neo Does

SlimBrave Neo writes Chromium [managed enterprise policies](https://chromeenterprise.google/policies/) to platform-specific locations. Brave reads these at startup and enforces them machine-wide — no browser modifications, no hacks.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="79 Policies" icon="shield-check">
    Seven categories covering telemetry, privacy, site permissions, access controls, Brave features, Shields, and performance.
  </Card>
  <Card title="6 Presets" icon="bookmark">
    Maximum Privacy, Balanced Privacy, Performance Focused, Developer, Strict Parental Controls, and Brave Origin.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Zero Dependencies" icon="circle-check">
    Python 3 stdlib only on Linux/macOS. PowerShell 5.1 (ships with Windows) on Windows. No pip, no npm, no brew.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
